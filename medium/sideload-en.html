Instagram's TV App Is "US-Only." I Sideloaded It in Taiwan — and It Just Works.

A 20-minute experiment to find out whether the region lock is a real geo-block, or just a Play Store label.

In February 2026, Instagram shipped an official Google TV app (com.instagram.airwave), but it's US-only — in Taiwan it's not even in the Play Store. But "not in the Play Store" and "won't work" are two different claims. One way to find out: sideload it and see.

Personal, educational curiosity. Sideloading third-party APKs carries its own risks — do it at your own discretion.

Step 1 — Pick the right APK

From APKMirror. The one detail that matters: match your device's CPU architecture. My 2020 Chromecast is 32-bit, so only armeabi-v7a installs; the arm64 build fails with INSTALL_FAILED_NO_MATCHING_ABIS. Check first:

adb shell getprop ro.product.cpu.abilist
# → armeabi-v7a,armeabi

Step 2 — Install over ADB

adb connect <tv-ip>:<port>
adb install com.instagram.airwave.apk   # → Success

Installed cleanly. No region check at install time.

Step 3 — Launch: no region wall

The moment of truth. Instead of "not available in your country," it went straight to the login screen.

Step 4 — Log in with a code, not a password

There's no password field on the TV. Like YouTube's TV login, it's a QR / device-activation flow: scan the QR, or enter the short code at instagram.com/activate. Your already-logged-in phone does the authentication.

The result: it fully works

After approving on the phone, the TV loaded the complete native app — a stories row, a "For you" grid of Reels, real content with real view counts. Zero region friction, front or back end.

Verdict: "US-only" is a Play Store listing restriction, not a technical geo-block. Sideload the APK, activate with a real account, and you get the full official experience outside the US.

The fine print

  • Sideloading isn't for everyone — most people just see "not available" and stop.
  • No auto-updates: off the Play Store, you update by re-sideloading.
  • It could change: a listing restriction today could become a real check tomorrow.

And honestly? This is exactly why I'd already built a scrappy WebView-wrapper Reels app for the same TV — before this experiment, "US-only" felt like a wall. Turns out it was a low fence.

Bonus: check the boring thing first

Half of my "it's broken, the screen is black!" panics were just the Chromecast's display being asleep (mWakefulness=Asleep). One adb shell input keyevent KEYCODE_WAKEUP and everything was fine.
